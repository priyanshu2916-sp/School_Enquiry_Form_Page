Frequently Asked Questions

What is the age criteria for Nursery admission?

The child should be at least 3 years of age as of 31st March of the admission year.

What documents are required for admission?

Birth certificate, transfer certificate (TC) from the previous school, last report card, and Aadhaar card copies.

Is school bus transport facility available?

Yes, CCTV and GPS-enabled school buses operate across all major city routes.

What are the school timings?

School timings are from 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM, Monday to Saturday.

How can we know about the fee structure?

Once you submit the enquiry form, our admission office will share the complete fee details with you.
